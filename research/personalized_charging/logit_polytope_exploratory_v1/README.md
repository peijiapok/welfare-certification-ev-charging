# Exploratory logit-polytope projection

This isolated prototype is a conservative, method-inspired construction for the synthetic fixed-AB two-driver charging model. It does not implement PRISM, Schnitzer et al.'s software, or establish benchmark superiority. No scientific catalog was run. All guarantees are conditional on the stated fixed common-shift logit model, its known contrast design and physics, predictable offer selection with fresh conditional Bernoulli responses, and correct directed arithmetic.

## Interface

`project_bands(bands, method='two'|'three')` takes a mapping for `p`, `r`, and (for three) `third`, each a two-element list of exact decimal strings or an existing interval object. `project_driver(counts, method=...)` takes `(successes, total)` integer pairs per stream; unsampled streams receive `[0,1]`. `evaluate({'A': counts_A, 'B': counts_B}, method=..., g0=..., g1=..., g2=..., W=..., delta=..., epsilon=...)` uses exact decimal strings for known physics and outputs `ALLOW` or `ABSTAIN`. Only A's p and B's r enter the AB deployment welfare/service bounds; all available per-driver contrasts can constrain each driver's theta. `vertices(constraints)` accepts rational inequalities `a*lambda + b*v <= c` for a bounded polygon and returns exact Fraction pairs; callers must supply the compact prior.

## Construction

For each driver define `lambda=1/tau`, `v=lambda*theta`, and `h=c+lambda*(0.1-theta)`. The logits for p, r, and third are respectively `h`, `h+v/2`, and `h+lambda/10`. The prior is `2.5 <= lambda <= 20` and `0 <= v <= lambda/4`; h has no artificial bound. Each finite lower logit endpoint implies `h >= L_j - offset_j` and each finite upper endpoint implies `h <= U_k - offset_k`. Eliminating h requires every lower bound to be no greater than every upper bound. Logit endpoints are first widened by the shared directed interval arithmetic, then converted to exact rational inequalities. No h constraint is needed when only lower or only upper endpoints exist.

Enumerate all pairwise boundary intersections with exact Fraction arithmetic, retaining feasible points. The prior makes the polygon compact, including line and point cases. Since lambda is positive, a convex combination's `v/lambda` is a lambda-weighted convex combination of vertex ratios, so minimum and maximum ratios occur at vertices. The reported rational endpoints are exact extrema of the **outward polygon**, which can be larger than the original feasible set. Decimal endpoints are widened again before use.

An outer polygon vertex is not a feasibility witness. The prototype tries its centroid and vertices, derives a finite h for each candidate, and checks that directed sigmoid intervals lie inside every original band. Failure to find a verified witness leads to abstention even when a real witness exists. Zero/one singleton bands are impossible under finite logits and rejected; one-sided bands and `[0,1]` retain their infinite logit sides without artificially bounding h. Computation outside the supported interval range also abstains.

The deployment gate retains the original outer p and r intervals and separately projected theta intervals; it does not optimize the full correlated welfare surface. It applies existing directed four-corner welfare and service bounds, requiring a strictly positive welfare lower bound and service upper bound at most epsilon. Empty outer regions, unverified feasibility, invalid input, or arithmetic failure abstain. This is conservative and can be powerless. No-data prior-only baseline is not implemented here.

## Confidence scope and limits

The shared two-driver probability bands allocate `alpha/6 = 1/120` to each of six streams; at sample count n the radius is `sqrt(log(240*n*(n+1))/(2*n))`. The sum over counts and streams gives a simultaneous failure probability at most 0.05 for both drivers under the model, including predictable reach and optional stopping; independence across streams is not required for this union bound. Using only four streams for the two-contrast variant gives the corresponding 4/120 bound. Different schedules, driver pairs, or model misspecification require their own error accounting; contrast-specific shifts invalidate the common-shift guarantee.

The projection and witness reuse the existing P3d interval arithmetic and band implementations, so they are not an independent arithmetic proof. There is no emitted replayable proof tree, hard time or memory cap, or independent solver check. Exact rational operation cost grows with endpoint size; the prototype imposes the imported count limit and may abstain on numerical or witness difficulties. Tests use deterministic constructed counts and exact rational geometry; they do not establish empirical power, real-world safety, or applicability to field charging.
